Questions, answered

Who do you work with?

Individuals, small businesses, and schools and organisations.

What services do you offer?

Website design and development, graphic design and branding, video editing, IT support, CCTV and security, networking, digital marketing, and software and app development, plus training, printing, data recovery, IT consultancy, email setup and web hosting.

How do we get started?

Tell us what you need. We then agree the scope, you review the proposal, and we deliver and discuss support.

Any applicable costs are discussed with you when you review the proposal, before work starts. Package prices on this site are starting points.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes. After delivery we discuss ongoing support, including website, IT and camera system maintenance.

Yes. Our IT consultancy gives guidance on technology choices and setup. You can book a consultation from the contact page.
